# Frameworks: Ken Tjandra

Built from his full Creattopia course transcript (S001 to S008). Scoring notes are guidance drawn from his own thresholds.

## 1. Storytelling over guruing *(his core approach)*
Tell what happened to you, the struggle, the wrestle, and the solution you lived, instead of telling people what to do. Use "I", not "you". Score: a draft that is mostly tips with no lived story scores low.
- "Don't be a guru, be a storyteller." (S001)
- Used in S001, S004, S005, S008.

## 2. 80/20 story to lesson
Spend about 80% of the video on the story, 20% on the lesson it earns. Score: lesson-first videos score low.
- "80% story, 20% lesson" (S001)

## 3. Ikigai (he calls it "icky guy")
Four boxes: passion (what you love), vocation (what you are good at), mission (how you serve others), profession (what you can be paid for). Circle forward from box to box, then write a mission statement and keep it super simple (KISS). Use it to find the niche and the brand.
- "Keep it super simple." (S001)

## 4. The Why framework
One to five "I believe" or "We believe" statements behind the mission, short and bold, from Simon Sinek's idea.
- "people follow your beliefs right not just your content" (S001)

## 5. Icon factors
Authenticity is now average; stand out by listing 5+ inner-child traits and 5+ mature-spirit traits and embracing all of them on camera, with maturity about the negative ones.
- "Nowadays, authenticity is the norm." (S001)

## 6. Dream avatar
Your younger self. Write a profile, then 10 hyper-specific dreams each in health, wealth, relationships, the macro and micro pains under each, and the solutions you actually lived. Speak to that one person. Score: generic audience language scores low.
- "Talk to one person." (S004)
- "Stick to the younger version of you." (S004)
- Used in S004, S005.

## 7. Hook, Retain, Reward
Hook in 1 to 3 seconds with a dream or pain; retain with structure, rhythm, story, and pattern breaks; reward with an emotional, spiritual, or practical payoff, sometimes as value drops through the video.
- "if your hook is weak, nothing else matters." (S005)
- "Your hook should also target a dream or a pain." (S005)

## 8. Heavy hooks and hook templates
Stack time frame, profile, dream, and pain into one line said in one take ("Is it possible to [dream] in [time] without [pain]?").
- Used in S005.

## 9. AAA rule
Appealing (looks and sounds interesting), Effective (makes you feel something), Authentic (real and credible). All three or the video fails.
- "Every viral piece of content hits all three of these." (S001)
- "Even one missing a can ruin your video." (S001)

## 10. Triple C
Colors, cinema, consciousness: the three that make a brand "one of zero".
- "Your colors grab the attention. Your cinema keeps them watching. your consciousness makes them stay." (S001)

## 11. VVA feedback check
Visual, verbal, auditory: does it look good, say one clear thing, sound clean? Rewatch your own draft at low volume like a scroller.
- "you should be watching your own content more than anything else" (S001)

## 12. Mood method
Match the script's tone to your real mood and enhance it two times.
- "Don't fake the hype and don't script against your spirit." (S005)

## 13. Pattern breaks
Interrupt your own flow: a joke, a face, a silence, cut music. About one every 7 to 10 seconds.
- Used in S005, S007.

## 14. Two-second rule and the fluff test
Change the visual every 1 to 2 seconds (never past 5); cut any line whose removal would not change the video; overlap the next line's audio a few milliseconds early.
- "If I remove this sentence or line, will my video change?" (S007)
- "every second counts" (S005)

## 15. More, Better, New
Reps in three stages: more (volume), better (quality, only when you can guarantee 10x), new (experiment). From Hormozi, applied to content.
- "if you can't guarantee 10x quality don't do it." (S008)

## 16. Content series
Five to seven episodes tied to one mission from your ikigai and current life, with a short name, planned with six questions (what you want to learn, what viewers should feel, one challenge, what you are curious about, life changes, the impact).
- Used in S008.

## 17. Consume and synthesize
80% creating, 20% consuming. Weekly self-analysis at the average-view-duration drop-off and competitor analysis, each turned into a short implementation checklist.
- Used in S002.

## 18. Scripting formulas
Tryhard (word for word plus screenplay), efficient (word-for-word hook, mixed script), casual (dot points), and freestyle.
- Used in S005, S008.
