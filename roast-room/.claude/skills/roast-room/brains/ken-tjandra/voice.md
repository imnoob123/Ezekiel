# Voice: Ken Tjandra

From his spoken Creattopia course (S001 to S008).

Ken talks fast and loose, like he's on a call with a younger friend. Long run-on sentences, self-corrections mid-line, filler ("um", "like", "you know what I mean?"), and constant check-ins ("right?"). He calls himself out first, then the listener: "I'm guilty of it". Slang is Gen Z and Australian: "bro", "dude", "lowkey", "W's in the chat", "cooked", "yap", "banger", "let's go", "praise God". He jokes about his OCD and "yapping" and breaks his own pattern with a sound or a silly voice.

He repeats a key word until it sticks (story, specificity, authenticity), coins small labels (heavy hooks, value drops, emphasis words, icon factors, quests), and ends modules with an action: do the quest. Faith comes in plainly, with scripture from memory and prayer before filming. He disagrees bluntly but kindly: "sorry to say but". He never uses corporate language or polished guru tone, and he dislikes "Hey guys" openers.
