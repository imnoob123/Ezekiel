# Positions: Ken Tjandra

From his full Creattopia course (S001 to S008, recorded across 2024 and 2025; dated by when the course was compiled, about 2025).

- **Guru content:** Against. Saturated and built his own year of flops. "I genuinely think it's saturated." (S001)
- **Storytelling:** The main strategy. "your authority is your story" (S001)
- **Niche:** Find it from your ikigai, then niche down to a hyper-specific avatar (S001, S003).
- **Audience targeting:** Your younger self. "Your dream advertised the younger version of you" (S004)
- **Faith in business:** Be bold about Christ, never sell the gospel. "Gospel is free, man." (S001)
- **Authenticity:** Necessary but no longer enough; add icon factors (S001).
- **Trend hopping:** Rarely works. "from my experience it does not work or at least it rarely works" (S001)
- **Perfectionism:** Against, except in the idea. "We are very anti-perfectionism." (S001)
- **Posting volume:** Start with more; daily reps; series of 5 to 7 (S008).
- **Batching vs daily:** Batch for consistency early, daily creating for quality later (S008).
- **Scripting:** Word-for-word hook always; dot points as you get comfortable (S005, S008).
- **Speaking pace:** Talk at about 1.5 times speed (S006).
- **Editing:** Cut every 1 to 2 seconds, manual one-to-two-word captions (S007).
- **Hashtags:** Barely matter. "the hashtags don't go viral, the content does." (S008)
- **Scheduling vs manual posting:** Moved to manual posting on Instagram at highest quality (S008).
- **Thumbnails:** Worth it for series on Instagram, optional elsewhere (S001).
- **Brand colors and fonts:** Two to three colors, one font, keep them 6 to 12 months (S001).
- **Views and money:** Views are not the goal. "views does not equal money." (S005)
- **Monetization:** Pushed to later course phases; profession box of the ikigai, coaching, courses, community (S001). Thin evidence.
- **AI:** Uses his own AI writing bot for feedback and formatting, not for ideas (S005).
- **Paid ads:** No recorded position.
- **Pricing and discounts:** Ran a 50% annual discount call to action for his community (S005). No recorded pricing philosophy.
- **Hiring, team, raising money:** No recorded position.
- **Burnout and breaks:** Took breaks and used "new" stages to reset (S008). Low evidence.
- **Analytics:** Find the second viewers leave and fix that mistake next time (S002).
