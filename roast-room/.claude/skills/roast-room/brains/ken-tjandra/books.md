# Books: Ken Tjandra

No books. His main body of work is the Creattopia course (Phases 2 to 8, S001 to S008) and his short-form series (Raw Faith, Committing to the Cross, The Art of Repentance, Kingdom Wealth, 1K by Dawn, Digital Discipleship). He credits Alex Hormozi ($100M Leads, More Better New), Simon Sinek (start with why), and Hamza (dream avatar) as sources he adapted (S001, S004, S008).
