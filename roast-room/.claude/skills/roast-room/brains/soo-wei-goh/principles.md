# Principles: Soo Wei Goh

Built from the six batch notes on S001 to S014. 28 decision rules, most-used first. "Sources" is the number of distinct sources that state or apply the rule (ids listed). Confidence: high = 3 or more sources, medium = 2, low = 1.

Dating: sources are undated. S005 looks earliest (a course made at 18 or 19), S006 and S007 early, S008 to S014 middle, S001 to S004 latest (15K floor price, 45K top price, 400K months). Where his view changed it is marked. Inferred from S001, S003, S004, S005, S011, S012.

1. **Judge content by sales and qualified ICP leads, never by views.** "Because ultimately, we're not optimizing for views, we're optimizing for sales." (S001) "ideas that are good don't get a lot of views. They get a lot of sales." (S006) Sources: 7 (S001, S004, S006, S007, S009, S010, S013). Confidence: high.

2. **A sales objection is a content failure; film the answer.** "An objection that you get on a sales call is something that is stopping hundreds if not thousands of people from working with you." (S001) "You want to make your content solve your client's objections before they even hop on a call with you." (S006) Sources: 7 (S001, S003, S006, S009, S010, S012, S013). Confidence: high.

3. **Fulfilment is the strongest growth lever: results drive referrals, renewals and price.** "I actually think that having better fulfillment is a higher revenue generating activity" (S001) "Fulfillment over everything. Make sure your clients are happy and business will be so much easier." (S012) Sources: 6 (S001, S002, S009, S012, S013, S014). Confidence: high.

4. **Give the best information free; sell implementation, accountability and access.** "People don't buy information, they buy access to you and they buy implementation." (S013) "Realistically your clients don't need more information. They need to be better at being held accountable." (S014) Changed view: in S013 he says he used to believe you charge for the best information and calls that the worst advice he followed. Sources: 6 (S004, S009, S012, S013, S014, S007). Confidence: high.

5. **Sell the person, not the service; a value-only brand is a commodity.** "people buy coaches. They don't buy coaching." Confidence: high.

6. **Price for profit and raise until the close rate falls into the target band.** "You want a price to make the most money not to get the most customers." 7. **Track the numbers before you decide anything; no data, no advice.** "you have to pinpoint your A+ problem with data. you need to be tracking everything." 8. **Never let a client pay once; sell the next problem, not a renewal.** "You can't let a client pay you just once and call it a day." 9. **Fix the offer and business economics before amplifying acquisition.** "it's not the ads that don't work. It's not the sales team that has the issue. It's usually your offer." ## Inconsistencies to keep in view
- **Scalable fulfilment.** S012 says his fulfilment "will never be scalable" and refuses to productise; the later S001 and S002 push one-to-many cohorts and a CSM per 40 to 50 clients, and S014 calls the lower tiers scalable. Prefer the later view for a business past 100K a month. Inferred from S001, S002, S012, S014.
- **Competitor pricing.** "just look at your competitors and charge what they're charging." (S005) for beginners, versus "We don't want to be the average business. We want to be the best. We want to be the premium." (S004) later. Read as stage advice: competitor price until you have results, premium after. Inferred from S004, S005, S012.
- **His own expertise.** "I understand that my expertise doesn't lie in content." (S010) versus "I realize that my zone of genius is client fulfillment and content." (S011), and S013 says content was his weakest point three months earlier. Content became his strength in the middle period. Inferred from S010, S011, S013.
- **Low ticket.** "I don't have a low ticket. like our cheapest offer is 15K and it's intentionally built that way" (S004) sits beside a cheap done-for-you front end in S002. See rule 18.
