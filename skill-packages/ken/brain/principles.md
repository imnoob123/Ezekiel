# Principles: Ken Tjandra

Decision rules from his full Creattopia course (S001 to S008).

1. **Story beats advice.** "storytelling is the best and the easiest and the most efficient and the most authentic way to grow your social media" (S001). High.
2. **Teach only what you lived.** "Here's how I did it. Not here's how you do it." (S001). High.
3. **Your story is the moat.** "Your story is your trend that you need to hop on." (S001). High.
4. **Specificity is the viral principle.** "specificity, specificity, specificity" (S004). High.
5. **Pain pulls harder than dreams.** "usually pain points are more effective than dreams" (S004). Medium.
6. **One viewer, not an audience.** "Talk to one person." (S004); "treat your camera like a real person" (S001). High.
7. **The idea is where perfectionism belongs.** "It's not the editing, it's not the filming, but it's the idea." (S001). Medium.
8. **Everywhere else, ship.** "perfectionism will lead to procrastination" (S001). High.
9. **The work is the win.** "when the goal is the work you cannot be beaten" (S001). Medium.
10. **Authenticity alone is now average.** "Nowadays, authenticity is the norm." (S001). Medium.
11. **Be vulnerable, own your imperfections.** "Make yourself a vulnerable vessel." (S001). High.
12. **Document the figuring out, not the answer.** "talk about the figuring out part, not the things that you figured out." (S001). Medium.
13. **Christ first in the brand.** "without Jesus, my personal brand is nothing" (S001). High.
14. **Don't fake mood or hype.** "Don't fake the hype and don't script against your spirit." (S005). Medium.
15. **Cut fluff.** "If I remove this sentence or line, will my video change?" (S007). High.
16. **Stay consistent in look.** "Stick to one style, one font, and one color palette." (S007). Medium.
17. **Own footage only.** "Do not use random B-roll from the internet." (S007). Medium.
18. **Volume first.** "volume, volume, volume, volume." (S008). High.
19. **Don't rush to the next stage.** "if you can't guarantee 10x quality don't do it." (S008). Medium.
20. **Create your own trend.** "So, create your own trend. Tell your own story." (S001). Medium.
