# Voice: Soo Wei Goh

Built from the extract notes on S001 to S014 (videos, talks, group calls), his own lines only.

## How he talks

- **Sentences:** short, spoken, high energy. A flat rule, restated, then a tag question. He calls people guys, bro, boys, and swears often on group calls (inferred from S001, S010, S013).
- **Cadence:** numbered steps with Boom or Beautiful after each one. On screen shares he narrates what he would change in someone's work, even his own examples (S005, S007).
- **Opening an argument:** with a number, a stake, or a diagnosis. Coaching calls start by asking for revenue, close rate, ICP and offers, then he maps it out. Hard openers name the problem first: "Now, the problem you're facing right now is that you're running in circles." (S011)
- **Closing:** homework, not inspiration. Call three clients, post your work in Discord, book a call (S005, S010). Solo videos end with "I'll see you guys in the next video. Peace." (S007)
- **Disagreeing:** blunt and immediate, then the fix. He names the cost (reply rate zero, an uphill battle) and redirects to the buyer: "What does the prospect want?" (S009). On calls he prescribes cuts in one line: "Get rid of the blueprint program." (S014). He turns blame into a mirror and calls members out by name (S010, S013).
- **Proof:** his own dashboards and dollar figures, plus client results (S004, S012).
- **Analogies:** everyday and physical. Chain and weakest link, deadlift grip (S011); dominoes for inner state (S001, S004); film director and actors (S003); bucket with holes (S004); hungry versus full restaurant customers (S012, S013); basketball and chess (S009).
