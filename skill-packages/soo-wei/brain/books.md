# Books: Soo Wei Goh

Built from the six batch notes on S001 to S014. He has written no books. His teaching lives in YouTube videos, group calls and course modules (Consulting Mastery). Below are the books he names and how he uses them, then his main video series. Summaries are in my own words; no book text is reproduced.

## Books he names

### $100M Offers (Alex Hormozi, 2021)
- **Core thesis:** Make an offer so good people feel stupid saying no, by raising perceived value instead of cutting price.
- **What he takes from it:** the value equation (dream outcome times perceived likelihood, divided by time delay times effort); bonuses worth more than the core offer; guarantee types; niching down to raise price. His own twist is to push time delay and effort toward zero first with done-for-you parts, then raise the top with client results. He credits the bonus rule directly: "I learned this from Horoszi, right? His $100 million offer book." (S012) and in the early course: "And this is in 100 million offers." (S005)
- **Taught in sources:** S005, S012, S013 (offer modules). Frameworks 15, 19 and 24 in frameworks.md.

### $100M Leads (Alex Hormozi, 2023)
- **Core thesis:** Get more leads by doing more of the core lead activities (warm and cold outreach, content, ads) and giving value first.
- **What he takes from it:** he names it beside $100M Offers as the two books that solve what to sell and how to get leads, and he says he copied a Hormozi-style funnel (proof, education, beliefs and identity content at the top, free best information and DM freebies in the middle, pre-sold calls at the bottom). "Look at his two books, right? $100 million offers and $100 million leads." (S013)
- **Taught in sources:** S013. His single-constraint and "do more until more stops working" ideas in S011 are also credited to Hormozi, though not to a named book.

### Building a StoryBrand (Donald Miller, 2017)
- **Core thesis:** The customer is the hero of the story; the brand is the guide that gives a plan and calls them to act.
- **What he takes from it:** the hero and guide journey for middle-of-funnel content and the VSSL; he uses it to argue against "value, value, value" content. He said he was about three quarters through it. "a book that goes through this is building a story brand by Donald Miller" (S006)
- **Taught in sources:** S006. Shapes the VSSL in S007.
